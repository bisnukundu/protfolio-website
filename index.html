Common questions

Do you work with clients in Europe?

Yes. Most of my clients are in Europe, especially Spain. I have been working with a Barcelona-based agency since 2020, and we communicate over WhatsApp, email or video call.

How long does a website take?

A simple business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Online shops and custom applications take 6 to 12 weeks or more depending on features. You get a timeline estimate after our first conversation.

Can you redesign my existing WordPress website?

Yes. I redesign existing WordPress sites with a modern look, better speed and a clearer path for visitors to contact you or buy, without losing your content.

Do you build WooCommerce online shops?

Yes. I build WooCommerce stores with product catalogues, payments, shipping options and the checkout experience your customers expect.

Will you help after the website is live?

Yes. I offer maintenance and support, including updates, backups, security checks, bug fixes and new features.
