Questions about wordpress developer

How much does a WordPress website cost?

It depends on pages, design and features. I give a fixed quote after understanding your project, so you know the price before we start.

Can you redesign my current WordPress site?

Yes. I keep your content and improve design, speed and structure, so visitors find what they need and contact you.

Which page builder do you use?

I work with Elementor and Elementor Pro, and also build custom themes in PHP when a project needs it.

Can you fix my broken WordPress site?

Yes. I debug errors, plugin conflicts, layout problems and email delivery issues, then explain what went wrong.

Yes. Every site I build is responsive and tested on desktop, tablet and phone.
