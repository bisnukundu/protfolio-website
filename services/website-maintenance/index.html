Signs your site needs maintenance

Small problems become lost customers. If any of these sound familiar, it is time to act.

  • Your site feels slowVisitors leave when pages take too long, and search engines notice.
  • Updates keep getting postponedOutdated plugins and themes are a common way WordPress sites get hacked.
  • Forms or checkout stopped workingEvery broken form or payment step is a lost enquiry or sale.
  • Strange pop-ups, redirects or warningsThese can be signs of malware that needs cleaning quickly.
  • Nobody on your team looks after the siteA developer on call means problems get fixed instead of ignored.

Questions about website maintenance and support

What does website maintenance include?

It covers software updates, backups, security checks, speed monitoring, bug fixes and small content changes, depending on the plan we agree.

Do I need maintenance if my site works fine?

Yes. Updates and security checks prevent problems before they appear. Outdated plugins and themes are a common way WordPress sites get hacked.

Can you maintain a website you did not build?

Yes. I review the site first, then take over updates, backups and support.

Do you maintain WooCommerce stores?

Yes. I test checkout, payments and order emails after updates so your shop keeps selling.

How much does maintenance cost?

It depends on the size of the site and how much support you need. Message me and I will suggest a plan with a clear monthly price.

What if my site has been hacked?

Contact me right away. I can help clean the site, restore a backup and close the security gap.
