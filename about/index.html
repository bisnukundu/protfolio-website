My story

I'm Bishnu Kundu, also known as Bisnu K on Fiverr. I'm a web developer with a strong foundation in both front-end and back-end technologies, and I love creating websites that are simple to use, fast and genuinely useful.

Since April 2020 I have worked as a freelance web developer with a Barcelona-based agency, building e-commerce sites, business websites and portfolios for Spanish-speaking businesses. I also take on direct projects through Fiverr and Freelancer.

My main tools are WordPress, WooCommerce, Elementor, PHP, JavaScript, React, Vue.js and Laravel. I write clean, maintainable code and I care about clear communication, honest timelines and long-term relationships with clients.

Outside client work I keep learning, publish open-source code on GitHub, and share web development tutorials on YouTube.
